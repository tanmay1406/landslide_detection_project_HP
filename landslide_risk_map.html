A Random Forest classifier trained on elevation, slope, terrain roughness, rainfall (7/15-day), distance to roads/rivers/buildings, land use, and NDVI. Between sampled points, terrain values are reconstructed by interpolation, so risk zones are smoothed estimates, not per-address measurements.

Read before using

This is a research and educational demo, not an official hazard assessment. It was built from a public modeling project and has not been reviewed or endorsed by any government or geological authority.

Model accuracy is approximately 68% on held-out test data — meaning roughly 1 in 3 classifications may be wrong. Do not use this map to make evacuation, construction, or other safety-critical decisions.

For official landslide hazard information, contact the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), the Geological Survey of India (GSI), or your local district administration.

Built from an open-source landslide susceptibility modeling project covering Himachal Pradesh's 12 districts. Ten model families were compared (Bagging, Boosting, MLP, CNN); this map uses the strongest performer, a 3-class Random Forest. District boundaries: 2011 Census via udit-001/india-maps-data (community-curated, verify for official use). Base map: OpenTopoMap / OpenStreetMap contributors.
